# THE NOTEBOOK
a working-class young man named Noah Calhoun and a wealthy heiress named Allie Hamilton who fall deeply in love during a summer in 1940s South Carolina, only to be separated by class expectations, hidden letters, and World War II.

Noah goes off to fight in World War II and later returns home to restore an old plantation house he once dreamed of sharing with Allie. Meanwhile, Allie is engaged to a wealthy, successful man named Lon Hammond Jr. After seeing a newspaper article about Noah’s restored home, Allie visits him. Reconnecting reignites her feelings for Noah, forcing her to choose between her fiancé and her first love

1. The Nursing Home Frame. In the present day, an elderly man named Duke reads a love story from a worn notebook to a fellow nursing home resident suffering from Alzheimer's disease.
2. A Summer Romance. Back in the 1930s/1940s, poor mill worker Noah Calhoun meets wealthy heiress Allie, and the two fall deeply in love over one passionate summer.
3. Forced Separation. Allie's parents disapprove of Noah's working-class background and force them apart. Noah writes to Allie faithfully, but her mother intercepts and hides every single letter, leading Allie to believe Noah forgot her.
4. Moving On. Years pass, and Noah serves in World War II before returning home to restore a dilapidated plantation house they once dreamed of living in together. Meanwhile, Allie gets engaged to Lon Hammond, a wealthy, charming lawyer.
